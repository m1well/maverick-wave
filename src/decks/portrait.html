What it is

A weekend that starts at the trailhead, not in traffic.

We meet at the station on Saturday morning. Twelve people at most, two guides, one night in a mountain hut.

The weekend

Four moments, one arc

  1. Station, 8:30

    Coffee, a short briefing, poles for anyone who wants them.

  2. The hut, 16:00

    Dorm or double room, dinner at a long table.

  3. Ridge, 6:00

    Head torches on, sunrise from the top.

  4. Valley, 15:00

    A swim in the lake, then the train home.
