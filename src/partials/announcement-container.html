Announcement

The ribbon glued under the fixed header - a discount, a launch note, a maintenance window. Shown here with mw-announcement-static, so the demos sit in the flow instead of over this page. Without it the ribbon fixes itself below the header, stays put while the page scrolls beneath it, and reserves the anchor scroll offset via --mw-announcement-height. The surface is 75% opaque with a backdrop blur - visible wherever content sits behind it, like the first demo below.
