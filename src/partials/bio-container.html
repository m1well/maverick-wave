Link in Bio

The page an Instagram profile links to, as part of the real site instead of a third-party page: the same header with a way back to the start, then one section. mw-bio goes on that section and keeps its container phone-wide on every screen, with a soft glow in the brand colours behind it. Inside, mw-bio-head carries the ringed mw-bio-avatar, the name, a tagline, an optional mw-bio-status for what is true today and a row of mw-bio-social icons; mw-bio-links stacks the cards, with an mw-bio-label between two groups. Head and cards rise in one after another on load.

The cards are mw-card mw-card-link on an <a>: an mw-card-link-media in front - an icon in the addon colour, -logo on a white plate or -photo as a thumbnail - then the title and an optional mw-card-link-subtitle. A title on its own sits centred on the card, a second line ranges the text left. The mark at the end is a chevron for a page of the site and an arrow out for a link with rel="external", target="_blank" or an http address. mw-card-link-cover puts a picture above, mw-card-link-featured fills the one link that matters most and sends a streak of light across it once the page has settled, and mw-card-link-quiet is the imprint at the bottom - flat, but at full contrast, since a legal notice may be quiet but not hard to find.
