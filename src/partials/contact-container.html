Contact

The practical block at the end of a site - when we are open, how to reach us. Only the second half is a component: mw-contact is an <address> with its italics undone and a label column that keeps the values under each other. The hours are a <dl> of mw-leader-row, because a day and its times with a line between them is the row that component already is.
