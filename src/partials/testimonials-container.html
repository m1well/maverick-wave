Testimonials

A quote card with its source - a figure, because quote and attribution belong together. One voice may stand above the grid as mw-testimonial-featured; the rest flow in mw-columns-3, the masonry layout for cards of unequal height. The cards below also carry mw-reveal - scroll them into view to see the entrance. Three of them are dated: mw-testimonial-date is a time element on the right edge of the source line, month and year. It is optional on purpose - a dated quote ages, so only show it where the voices are kept fresh.
