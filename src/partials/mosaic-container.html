Mosaic

Photos of any shape in one grid. The script reads each photo's proportions from its width and height and gives it a tile to match - wide for landscape, tall for portrait, square for the rest - and the smaller tiles fill the holes. The tile crops, the lightbox does not: a tap opens the photo whole with its caption, and a swipe moves on to the next one. mw-mosaic-gap-sm and mw-mosaic-gap-lg change the gaps.
