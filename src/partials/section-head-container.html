Section Head

The opening of a section: a hairline rule, a mark sitting on its left end, the title, and the line that says what follows. Everything starts at one edge - centred body copy moves its own left edge with every line break, so the eye has to find the start of each line instead of jumping to a fixed position. Fine for a heading, tiring from the third line on.

Numbered

mw-section-head-numbered counts in the browser, so no page keeps its own running order in the markup. The number closes the rule the mark opens, and the counter restarts per mw-main. Opt-in: a single section on a legal page numbered "01" is noise.

Colored Mark

The mark is the one colored thing in the head, so it is where a section picks up a tone: mw-section-head-secondary, -success, -warning, -danger and -info. Its length is --mw-section-head-mark-width (80px), the gap to the content below --mw-section-head-gap. That gap and the space above the title are multiples of --mw-section-head-step, which follows --mw-section-padding-block - so the section spacing picker in the palette panel moves the heads along with the page.
