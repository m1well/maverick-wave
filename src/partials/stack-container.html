Stack

Cards that pile up while the page scrolls - for a handful of projects or case studies that each deserve a row of their own. From lg every child of mw-stack stops below the fixed header, one step lower than the one before, and the next slides over it. On a phone they stay a plain stack: a card taller than the screen would never show its own end. The step is --mw-stack-step, the offset counts up to the twelfth card, and the children need a background of their own - a card has one.
