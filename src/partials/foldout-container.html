Foldout

A text that goes on after "read more". What stands before mw-foldout-body is always visible; the body opens like an accordion panel and the toggle swaps its label by state. Nothing is measured or clamped - cut the text where a sentence ends and put the rest in the body. In a grid of cards add mw-items-start, or the neighbours of an opened card stretch along and show their empty surface beside it.

In running text

The toggle keeps one label when it holds plain text instead of the two spans - fine for a text that is opened once and read to the end.

The body takes any content: paragraphs, a list, a picture. The inner wrapper is the animated row and is not optional - content placed straight into the body never collapses when shut.
