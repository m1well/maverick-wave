Simple Pagination

The same frame as a calendar - an arrow on each side of the title, content below - but the body is yours. Not a page-number list; the paging itself is your job, mw-pagination-loading dims the body while the next page is on its way.

Numbered Pages

mw-pagination-pages for a list or a table. aria-current="page" marks where you are, aria-disabled="true" an arrow that leads nowhere. On a phone the numbers give way to mw-pagination-status between the arrows.
