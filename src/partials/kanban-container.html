Kanban

Default Board

A board is a mw-kanban with one mw-kanban-column per status. The board is a grid that gives every lane the same width no matter how many there are.

A ticket is a plain mw-card with mw-kanban-card on top – title, text and a footer holding the key, the assignee and four actions: move one lane left or right, edit, delete. Its priority is the regular mw-card-ribbon, which already brings its colour along through mw-card-addon-danger|warning|info; a ticket without a priority simply has no ribbon.

Lanes are as tall as the fullest one, with --mw-kanban-column-min-height as the floor – 120px by default, 90px on a compact board. Raise it per board (style="--mw-kanban-column-min-height: 280px") so a board that starts out empty still reads as a board.

Descriptions are clamped to four lines, two on a compact board, so one wordy ticket cannot dwarf the ones next to it.
