Device Frames

A laptop and a phone drawn in CSS, with a page on the glass - for showing a site the way people meet it, next to the prose that sells it. mw-devices stands the phone over the laptop's corner; each frame is an mw-device with mw-device-laptop or mw-device-phone and an mw-device-screen inside. A screenshot on the glass scrolls to its end while the pointer rests on the laptop, and a device written as a <button> tours it on a tap: to the end, a pause, and back.

An iframe instead of the picture shows the live page, laid out at --mw-device-viewport and scaled to the glass, so it scrolls by hand like the real thing. Both frames also stand on their own; the phone then takes a column of up to 22rem.

The same frames with a live site: m1well.com, laid out once at 1280px and once at 390px. Scroll the phone by hand, page through the laptop - nothing here is a picture.
