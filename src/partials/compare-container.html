Before & After

Two pictures of the same place on one frame, split where the reader drags - the renovated bathroom, the cleared garden, the restored facade. The control is a native range laid over the whole frame, so a finger, a mouse and the arrow keys all move it, and a screen reader announces the position. Without the script the frame shows a fixed half-and-half split.
