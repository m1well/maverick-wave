Segmented Control

Two or three positions of one switch - income or expense, offer or quote. Deliberately not a mw-button-bar: that one stacks below 576px and gives every button 96% width, which is right for a row of independent actions and wrong here. Stacked, two positions of a switch look like two buttons you could press both of. A segmented control stays horizontal at every width and splits the row into equal shares.
