Footer

The page footer at the bottom of this site is the full shape: columns, social links, a note and the copyright. Three pieces make it fit everything else. mw-footer-bottom puts the copyright at one end and mw-footer-links at the other - the imprint and the privacy policy a German site has to link from every page - and stacks both, centred, on a phone. mw-footer-dark turns the footer into the dark bookend of the header bar, and mw-footer-compact is the bar alone, the footer an application puts under its content.
