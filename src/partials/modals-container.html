Simple Modals

A <dialog> carrying the same mw-modal classes. Escape, the focus trap and the inert page behind it come from the element, the page scroll lock from the stylesheet - only opening it needs a line of script.

Modal Sizes

Examples

Drawers

mw-modal-drawer turns the same dialog into a full-height panel on the right edge, with mw-modal-drawer-start on the left. The body scrolls between a fixed header and footer, and on a phone it stays a side panel instead of becoming a sheet. Opened with show() instead of showModal() it sits beside the content and leaves the page usable - a copilot or a detail view.
